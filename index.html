MotoX VR

VRMeta QuestSteamLead Developer

MotoX is an immersive VR motocross racing game where players experience the speed and adrenaline of virtual motocross across 7 unique worlds in time trials.

The studio had released this title before I joined and it was not well received — rated 2.8 stars. I led a team through a complete remodel of the title which resulted in a drastic turnaround. Today MotoX has climbed to a 4-star rating, seen a 956% increase in revenue, and has a fast-growing fan base on TikTok.

VR Doctor Training Simulator: LMA Insertion

VRMedicalEducationLead Programmer

This VR prototype teaches individuals how to insert a Laryngeal Mask Airway (LMA). A freelance project with Dr. Zachery Tan and Dr. Alfonso Rodriguez Herrera from Kilkenny Hospital. I was the lead programmer.

Playtested by the National Ambulance Service and UCD medical students. Showcased at the 2020 IGBL Conference. Awards received:

  • Runner-up — Best Technical Achievement at the IMIRT 2019 Game Awards
  • Best Medical/Education Management Poster — IPA Conference 2020
  • Best Overall Oral Poster Presentation — IPA Conference 2020
